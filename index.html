THE TEN COMMANDMENTS OF LIBERTY STATE

Let it be known that the following commandments shall guide
all who call Liberty State their home.

THE LAWS OF OUR CITY I — X TO BE UPHELD BY ALL
I

1. THOU SHALT REMAIN IN CHARACTER AT ALL TIMES

Out of Character (OOC) communication is strictly prohibited during gameplay. Do not break character, reference Discord, staff, game mechanics, or real-life situations. Regardless of what happens, remain in character and allow the roleplay to continue. Any issues must be handled through the proper support channels after the scene has concluded.

II

2. THOU SHALT RESPECT EVERY PLAYER

Treat everyone with respect, regardless of their role, experience, or position within the city. Bullying, harassment, personal attacks, toxicity, rage baiting, and deliberately provoking players to cause arguments will not be tolerated. Keep conflict between characters, never between the people playing them.

III

3. THOU SHALT LET ROLEPLAY FLOW NATURALLY

Every interaction is an opportunity to create a story. Allow situations to develop naturally without rushing, forcing outcomes, or deliberately ruining scenes. Give other players the chance to speak, react, and contribute. Not every interaction needs to become a confrontation, chase, or shootout.

IV

4. THOU SHALT NOT SEEK TO WIN EVERY SCENE

Liberty State RP is about storytelling, not winning. Win hunting, unrealistic behaviour to gain an advantage, and refusing to accept reasonable consequences are prohibited. Sometimes you will win, sometimes you will lose, and sometimes the best roleplay comes from failure. Accept the outcome and continue your character's story.

V

5. THOU SHALT NOT USE INFORMATION YOUR CHARACTER DOES NOT KNOW

Metagaming is strictly prohibited. Information gained from Discord, streams, external communications, or anything your character has not legitimately discovered through roleplay must never be used in game. Your character only knows what they have learned within the city.

VI

6. THOU SHALT NOT FORCE ROLEPLAY UPON OTHERS

Powergaming and unrealistic actions are prohibited. Do not force outcomes, abuse game mechanics, or perform actions that deny another player a fair opportunity to respond. Roleplay must remain believable, with everyone involved having a reasonable chance to participate.

VII

7. THOU SHALT NOT KILL OR ATTACK WITHOUT VALID ROLEPLAY

Random Deathmatch (RDM) and Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM) are strictly prohibited. Violence must have genuine roleplay reasoning and appropriate escalation. Shooting, attacking, or using a vehicle as a weapon without valid roleplay justification is unacceptable. A minor disagreement is not automatically a reason to resort to deadly force.

VIII

8. THOU SHALT VALUE THY LIFE

Your character's life must be treated as valuable. FearRP and realistic self-preservation are expected. You must react appropriately to credible threats, dangerous situations, and overwhelming odds. Acting recklessly simply because you know you can respawn is not acceptable. Your character is a person, not an expendable game piece.

IX

9. THOU SHALT ACCEPT THE CONSEQUENCES OF THY ACTIONS

Do not combat log, deliberately disconnect, exploit respawns, or abandon active scenes to avoid arrest, injury, robbery, or other roleplay consequences. If a situation does not go your way, continue the scene. Police officers, criminals, business owners, and civilians are all expected to accept the consequences of their decisions.

X

10. THOU SHALT PROTECT THE INTEGRITY OF LIBERTY STATE

Exploiting bugs, cheating, abusing mechanics, deliberately disrupting roleplay, or taking advantage of another player's technical difficulties is forbidden. Do not retaliate against rulebreakers by breaking rules yourself. Complete the scene where possible, then report the issue through the appropriate channels. Help build a city where everyone can enjoy meaningful, fair, and immersive roleplay

Honour the code. Enrich the story.

By Order of Liberty State RP Management
